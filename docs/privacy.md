# Inside & Out Privacy Policy

Last updated: 03-10-2026

## About this policy

Inside & Out is a family organiser for inmate profiles, court dates, medical information, visits, calls, tasks and documents. It is not a government, correctional, legal or medical service. This policy describes how the app handles information. Contact Inside & Out at insideandoutapp.support@gmail.com with privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion problems or complaints.

## Information you choose to provide

Device profiles contain the names, inmate identifiers, locations, dates, notes, task details and files you enter or select. Shared family accounts also use your email address, account identifier, invitations, membership and activity history. Records may contain sensitive health, legal or family information about other people. Only add information you are authorised to hold and share. Avoid unnecessary identity documents, passwords and financial account details.

## Device profiles and app access

You can use device profiles without a family account. These records and attachments stay in app storage unless you export or explicitly share them. Your operating system or device backup settings may also create copies. The app PIN and family sign-in session are held in secure device storage on iOS and Android. Face ID or Touch ID is checked by the operating system; Inside & Out does not receive your biometric template. App Lock controls access to the app. Local records, attachments and exported files are not separately encrypted by Inside & Out.

## Family sharing

Signing into family access sends your email to our authentication service so a sign-in code can be delivered. Choosing to share a profile uploads its records to cloud storage. Files are uploaded when you select them for a shared profile. Each shared inmate has one admin. All invited members who join that profile can read its shared records and documents and update task completion. The admin manages records, uploads and access. Members can see family email addresses and activity attribution. There are no document-by-document privacy controls. Your original device profile remains a separate copy and does not synchronise with the shared profile.

## Why information is used

Information is used to save and display your records, authenticate family accounts, control access, synchronise shared changes, deliver sign-in codes, record family activity, respond to support requests and protect the service from misuse. The app does not sell personal information, contain advertising or use advertising-tracking SDKs. It does not automatically read your phone call history, contacts, messages or government booking records. Calls and appointments are entered by you. Reminders are scheduled on your device if you grant notification permission.

## Service providers and location

Supabase provides family authentication, database and private file storage. The app’s database project is hosted in Sydney, Australia. Google email services deliver sign-in messages and handle correspondence sent to our support address. Apple, Google and Expo provide app distribution, operating-system or build services; their own policies apply to information they collect. Network services may process IP addresses, request timestamps, errors and security logs. Provider support, email and operational processing may occur outside Australia. A Sydney database region is not a promise that every associated service processes data only in Australia. Authorised operators and providers may access information where needed to operate, secure or support the service.

## Documents, exports and access removal

Shared files use a private storage bucket with checks for the selected inmate’s admin or family membership. File downloads use time-limited links. Anyone with a still-valid link may use it until it expires. Removing a member prevents new authorised access, but cannot recall a file, screenshot or export they already obtained. PDF, CSV and JSON exports contain readable information. You choose where exports are saved or sent and who receives them. The app attempts to remove its temporary export copies after sharing; Android uses a short grace period and startup cleanup. Copies saved in another app, browser or backup are outside this cleanup. Do not treat shared documents as end-to-end encrypted.

## Retention and deletion

Device records remain until you clear them or remove the app’s data. Older versions may retain an earlier local recovery copy; uninstalling or clearing app storage removes app-held copies, but device backups and exports can remain. Shared records remain until their admin deletes them. In Family access, choose Delete my family account. For every profile you administer, choose an existing member to become its sole admin or permanently delete the profile and shared documents. The app requires typed confirmation. Transfers preserve family records. Profile deletion removes shared access immediately, then the service removes the files and account. If cleanup is interrupted, retry the request; a saved deletion request is not a completed deletion.

## What account deletion does not erase

Account deletion removes your memberships and invitations to your email and anonymises account attribution in retained family activity. Records kept by another admin, including a profile you transfer, may still contain your name or information in their text or documents. Contact that admin or support for help with those records. Separate On this device profiles, downloaded copies and exports are not deleted with a family account. Where provider backups or operational logs exist, older copies may remain according to the provider’s retention settings or legal obligations; they are not available as live family records. Contact support for help if your app version lacks account deletion or you cannot complete the process.

## Your choices and concerns

You can keep records on your device, decline optional notifications, edit your information, ask a profile admin to correct or remove shared information, export records, remove family access as admin, or delete your family account. Contact insideandoutapp.support@gmail.com to request access or correction, report unwanted sharing, or raise a privacy complaint. Include only the minimum information needed; do not send your PIN or sign-in codes. We may need to verify your identity before acting. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your phone protected and invite only people you trust.

## Policy updates

We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The current version is available inside the app and through the public Privacy policy link. Check the last-updated date. New payment, advertising or integration features will require their own privacy review before being enabled.
