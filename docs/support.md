# Inside & Out Support

Inside & Out is a family organiser for keeping inmate-related dates, tasks, interactions and documents together.

## Contact

Email: insideandoutapp.support@gmail.com

Please do not send your app PIN, email sign-in codes, passwords, banking details or unnecessary identity documents.

## Common help

### I cannot get back to the email code screen
Update to the latest TestFlight/App Store version. The current sign-in flow preserves your email and reopens the code-entry step after you leave the app.

### A family member cannot see a shared profile
Ask the inmate profile's admin to confirm the invitation email, then sign in with that same email address and open Family access.

### I lost access to a shared profile
The inmate profile admin controls membership. Removed members cannot regain access without a new invitation.

### I need to delete my family account
Open Family access → Delete my family account. For every profile you administer, either transfer admin to an existing family member or permanently delete that profile and its shared documents, then type the confirmation phrase.

### I need privacy help
See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.md) or email insideandoutapp.support@gmail.com.
